# RazorRecover AI

### Autonomous AI Revenue Recovery & Smart Dunning Engine

> **RazorRecover AI** is an agentic revenue recovery platform that detects revenue at risk, diagnoses payment failures, selects the next-best recovery action, validates it through deterministic guardrails, and measures the revenue recovered.

**Built for Razorpay /buildathon — Track 03: AI Revenue Recovery**

---

## 🚀 Why RazorRecover?

Revenue loss rarely happens as a single failure.

A payment can fail because of a temporary bank or PSP issue.  
A recurring mandate can fail because of insufficient balance at the wrong time.  
A customer can abandon checkout during authentication.  
A B2B invoice can remain overdue for weeks.

Traditional systems are good at detecting these events.

The harder problem is:

> **What should happen next?**

RazorRecover AI closes that gap with an autonomous recovery workflow:

```text
Detect → Diagnose → Decide → Validate → Execute → Measure
